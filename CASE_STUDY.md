ArcTrade — Stock Trading & Investment Platform Case Study

Overview

ArcTrade is a beginner-friendly stock trading and investment app designed to simplify investing while providing powerful tools for active traders and long-term investors. The goal was to create a seamless experience for users to invest, track portfolios, monitor markets, and manage wealth from a single platform.

---

Problem Statement

Many investment platforms are either too complex for beginners or lack advanced tools for experienced investors. New users often struggle with financial jargon, portfolio tracking, and making informed investment decisions.

ArcTrade was designed to make investing accessible, educational, and confidence-building while maintaining professional trading capabilities.

Key Findings

- Users prefer simple onboarding.
- Portfolio tracking should be easy to understand.
- Educational guidance increases confidence.
- Fast stock search and trading are essential.
- Personalized insights improve engagement.

Design Decisions

- Mobile-first design approach.
- Clean and minimal interface.
- Blue-based color palette to establish trust.
- Bottom navigation for easy access.
- Beginner Mode to explain financial terms.
- AI-powered investment insights.
- Portfolio Health Score for diversification tracking.

Design System

Learnings

- Simplicity is critical in financial products.
- Educational content reduces investment anxiety.
- Clear information hierarchy improves decision-making.
- Personalized insights increase user engagement.
- Balancing beginner-friendly experiences with advanced tools creates a broader user appeal.
